Overview

Six short video ads for FarMart's two direct-to-consumer food brands, TrulySafe and Sugarwatchers, made with AI. Each ad picks one product and one truth, and tells it in under half a minute: safe, clean food for TrulySafe, and smarter, low-GI eating for Sugarwatchers.

TrulySafe

TrulySafe sells clean, tested everyday staples, so these ads are about trust. Warm home kitchens, golden light, and the yellow pack always in the frame, ending at the family table.

Sugarwatchers

Sugarwatchers makes low-GI versions of everyday foods. Here the tone gets brighter and more playful, from a blood-sugar chart for the atta to tiny chefs cooking millet noodles.

What every ad follows
  1. 01

    One product, one truth

    Each ad sells a single idea: safe and pure, low GI, high in fibre. Nothing else competes for the few seconds people give an ad.

  2. 02

    The pack is the hero

    The product pack appears in almost every shot, so the brand is remembered even with the sound off.

  3. 03

    Short, punchy supers

    On-screen lines stay to a few words, like “Veggies in. Aroma on.” or “Golden. Crispy. Effortless.”, readable at a glance on a phone.

  4. 04

    End at the table

    Most ads close on people eating together. That's the real promise of a food brand: a good meal at home.

  5. 05

    One idea that's just for fun

    The tiny chefs in the noodle ad show that AI can do more than polished product shots. It can make a playful idea cheap enough to try.
