FarMart Dashak

Branding for FarMart's 10-year anniversary: one identity for a two-day celebration, from ten-year walls to trophies.

ClientFarMart
Year2026
RoleCreative & Product Designer
DeliverablesEvent identity, walls, backdrops, flags, certificates, trophies, mementos, printables
TeamWith Akshay Sharma and Anjali Choudhary
Overview

FarMart Dashak was FarMart's tenth anniversary, a two-day, company-wide celebration. Day one was Sports Day; day two was the main anniversary function. Together with Akshay Sharma and Anjali Choudhary, I worked on the event's branding and visual design, taking one identity from ten-year walls and stage backdrops all the way down to certificates, trophies and mementos.

Two days, two moods
Day 1

Sports Day

Energetic

Sports, team activities and employee participation, with its own sports awards.

Day 2

Main function

Polished

The formal ten-year celebration: FarMart's journey, recognition and awards.

The challenge

The identity needed to:

  • Celebrate the ten-year milestone
  • Work across both days of the event
  • Feel energetic for Sports Day and polished for the main function
  • Stay consistent across printed and physical materials
  • Scale from a large wall down to a certificate or trophy
A complete branded experience, not a collection of event assets.
Mapping the two days

I started by mapping what each day needed: the activities, the tone, and the awards. That showed where the identity had to stay fixed and where it could flex for the context.

I worked with FarMart's marketing and event teams throughout, from understanding requirements and exploring directions to incorporating feedback and preparing final files for production.

One system, every size

The same visual language had to hold up across very different formats, each seen at a different distance:

  1. 10-year wallsSeen from across the venue
  2. Backdrops and stageRead in photos and from the audience
  3. Flags and venue brandingGlimpsed while moving through the space
  4. Event printablesPicked up and read
  5. CertificatesRead up close, kept
  6. TrophiesHeld, photographed
  7. MementosTaken home

Large-format pieces had to work from a distance, while certificates, trophies and mementos needed finer, more refined detail. Everything was designed in Figma with physical production in mind.

Key decisions
  1. 01

    Make “10 Years” the hero

    The milestone became the central visual element and the common thread connecting every asset and both days.

  2. 02

    One identity, two experiences

    Instead of separate branding for each day, a shared system that adapts, so the days feel connected while keeping their own character.

  3. 03

    Take the identity off the wall

    The branding extended into certificates, trophies and mementos, so the identity became something employees could take home.
